A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Day or night, Sherman Restoration responds to burst pipe emergencies across Sherman and the nearby communities. Our restoration technicians can help you locate and shut off your water supply, and we coordinate with licensed plumbers so the plumbing repair is done properly. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

Signs You Need Burst Pipe Repair in Sherman

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes
  • Faucets that give only a trickle, or nothing, during a freeze
  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • A sudden drop in water pressure throughout your property
  • Water charges that jump for no clear reason, a sign of a concealed leak
  • A stained, soggy or bowed section of drywall overhead or beside you
  • The sound of running water when no fixtures are in use

The Benefits of Professional Burst Pipe Repair

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. It moves between ceiling joists, inside floor assemblies, beside electrical conduit and down wall cavities, soaking materials you might not find for days or even weeks. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. A truck-mounted unit clears water at hundreds of times the speed of a shop vac, while a commercial dehumidifier takes 10 to 20 times more moisture out of the air than a household model. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • Put leak sensors by the water heater and along the main supply line
  • Going away in cold months? Keep the thermostat running to protect the plumbing from freezing
  • Replace old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes before they fail unexpectedly
  • Everyone in the house should be able to locate and turn the main water valve in a hurry
  • A plumbing inspection by a licensed pro every few years catches corrosion and wear early
