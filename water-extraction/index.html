Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Sherman Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout Sherman using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

Removing visible water is only part of what professional extraction involves. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Place leak detection sensors in the key risk areas and test that they work
  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number
  • Regularly inspect under sinks, around toilets and near appliances for early signs of a leak
  • Maintain appliance supply lines and replace hoses that show wear or age
  • Install automatic water shutoff valves that detect leaks and stop the flow
  • Keep valuables and stored boxes off the basement floor on shelves or pallets

How to Tell You Need Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water accumulating after a fire suppression system discharges
  • Rising water in the basement because the sump pump stopped working
  • Saturated carpet that squishes underfoot or shows water pooling
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • Drips or a steady stream coming out of a ceiling, a wall or a fixture
  • Laminate or wood flooring that feels spongy and pushes up water at the seams

Why Water Extraction Is Not a DIY Job

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. This speed difference is not just about convenience, it is about preventing thousands of dollars in additional damage that occurs with every hour water stays in contact with building materials.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. We go after those hidden deposits with specialty tools and methods, and that takes out far more water than extracting the surface only. The payoff is a shorter drying period, lower equipment cost and, most important of all, less risk of mold growing unseen in trapped moisture.
